# Analysis

## Layer 5, Head 4
This attention head appears to capture **parallel structures formed with the correlative conjunctions** `not only … but also …`. 

**Example sentences:**
- _Not only is our system a [MASK] way to get in shape, but also a great way to save money!_
- _Not only do our chefs teach you how to cook, but they also teach you to [MASK] the best ingredients!_


## Layer 6, Head 7
This attention head seems to focus on **two structural blocks**:  
1. **go-constructions** such as `going there`, `go to …`, and  
2. **causal patterns** such as `because of / … is / … were`.

**Example sentences**:
- _She didn’t go [MASK] because there was a dog._
- _I’m [MASK] going there because it is raining._
